# License decision

Decision date: 2026-08-08. Release mode: verified-license-first, private visibility.

## Evidence

- The repository README declared MIT in the 2025-10-03 pre-event commit and retained that declaration through the competition history.
- MIT is OSI approved and fits an application intended for source redistribution.
- The exact Participant Terms accepted for the 2025 submission are not available from the mutable official legal page. Similar 2024 and 2026 clauses are context, not proof of the 2025 contract.
- The available 2025 submission/judging guides require originality, permission, external-resource disclosure, and AI-use disclosure.
- The complete team roster and each contributor's authority to license retained code have not been independently verified.
- The challenge-linked CSV and historical media are technically unnecessary and lack sufficient redistribution evidence.

## Decision

Use the official unmodified MIT text for original AIONEX application code with:

> Copyright © 2025 AIONEX and project contributors.

Do not claim sole ownership. Do not apply an inferred Apache-2.0 default. Keep all third-party rights intact, exclude the unlicensed CSV/media, and retain factual event attribution without logos or endorsement language.

## Publication condition

The private repository and private `v1.0.0` release may be used for technical review. Public visibility is not cleared until both conditions are documented:

1. the exact accepted 2025 Participant Terms or written organizer clarification; and
2. authority to publish all retained contributor code under MIT.

If either source imposes a conflicting obligation, reassess the files and license before changing visibility. The evidence trail and source links are in [COMPETITION_AND_RIGHTS.md](COMPETITION_AND_RIGHTS.md).
